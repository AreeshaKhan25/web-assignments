Admission Requirements

Review the essential academic records and documents required before starting your online application submission.

  • Official academic transcripts (HSSC, FSc, A-Levels, or equivalent diploma certificates).
  • Standardized test scores (NTS-NAT / GAT or university-administered entrance test).
  • 500-word statement of intent highlighting research or career ambitions.
  • Two academic references from former instructors (required for graduate admissions).
